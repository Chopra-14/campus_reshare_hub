OVERVIEW

A secure campus ecosystem for item recovery & student resale.

Campus ReShare Hub is a smart digital platform that helps students recover lost belongings and safely buy or sell second-hand academic essentials — all within a verified college environment. Only students with an institutional email can access it, ensuring a trusted and safe community.
